---

## 🚩 Key Challenges & Pain Points:

**Pain point 1: Forgetting what they learned**

* They learn techniques in class but forget the details days later.
* Their notes are scattered across apps, notebooks, screenshots, and memory.
* They feel years of training are slipping away undocumented.

**Pain point 2: Not seeing the shape of their own game**

* They know they have habits, patterns, and holes, but cannot clearly see them.
* They struggle to connect individual sessions into a long-term arc.
* They want insight without turning training into a scoreboard.

**Pain point 3: Plateau and lack of direction**

* Blue belt/purple belt periods feel murky and repetitive.
* They train consistently but wonder what they are actually improving.
* They fear becoming someone who "just shows up" without intentional growth.

---

## 🌟 Goals & Aspirations:

**Short-Term Goals:**

* Remember what they worked on after each class.
* Track positions, partners, techniques, and reflections in one place.
* Identify recurring holes and themes in their training.

**Long-Term Aspirations:**

* Build a recognizable personal game over years.
* Look back on their BJJ journey like a meaningful record, not a blur.
* Still be training intelligently and sustainably decades from now.

## 📌 Typical Emotional Journey:

**Awareness:**
They start noticing that classes blur together, techniques disappear from memory, and their training has no durable record.

**Frustration:**
They feel plateaued, scattered, and unsure what they are actually building. Notes apps and notebooks help briefly but become inconsistent or hard to review.

**Desperation & Seeking Solutions:**
They search for ways to remember techniques, track training, identify holes, and become more intentional without using shallow fitness-style metrics.

**Relief & Commitment:**
They find a journal-like system that respects BJJ as a long-term practice, helps them record the details, and surfaces the story and patterns of their training over time.
