# Discovery Story

Classic structure:

---

I kept training.

Three times a week.
Sometimes four.

For years.

And one day I realized:

I couldn't remember half of what I learned.

Not because I was lazy.
Not because I wasn't paying attention.

Because training moves fast.

Weeks blur.

Months disappear.

Years stack up.

And your game becomes a fog of half-remembered positions.

So I started writing things down.

At first:

In my phone.
In notebooks.
In random notes.

But the real breakthrough wasn't recording sessions.

It was seeing patterns.

What I kept returning to.
What I avoided.
What never improved.
What slowly became mine.

That's when I realized:

The problem wasn't technique.

It was visibility.

And that's what became Journada.
